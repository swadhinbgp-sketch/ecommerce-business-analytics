# E-Commerce Business Intelligence & Customer Analytics

## 1. Business Objective

ShopSphere is an e-commerce company that wants to use its sales and customer data to improve revenue, profitability, customer retention, and product performance.

The objective of this project is to analyze historical e-commerce data and provide actionable insights to management.

## 2. Key Business Questions

### Sales Performance
- What is the total revenue?
- What is the total profit?
- How is revenue changing over time?
- Which regions generate the most revenue?

### Product Performance
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which products generate the highest profit?
- Which categories perform best?
- Which products are underperforming?

### Customer Analysis
- How many customers do we have?
- Who are our most valuable customers?
- What percentage of customers are repeat customers?
- Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
- Which customers may be at risk?

### Business Performance
- What are the major drivers of revenue?
- What are the major drivers of profit?
- Where are we losing potential revenue?
- What actions should management take?

## 3. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value
- Repeat Customer Rate
- Customer Lifetime Value
